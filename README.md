### 👋 About Us

Welcome to the official GitHub organization for **Novation**! We are a student-led initiative based at **Sri Lanka Technological Campus (SLTC)**, dedicated to exploring the frontiers of Artificial Intelligence. 

Our mission is to bridge the gap between academic theory and real-world innovation by bringing together the brightest minds in Sri Lanka.
